## 📽️ Step-by-Step Recording Script

### Scene 1: Introduction & Live Production App (0:00 - 0:25)
- 🖥️ **Action**: Open Google Chrome and navigate to `https://mexora.vercel.app`.
- 🗣️ **Script**: 
  > *"Welcome to Mexora — an autonomous AI intelligence platform that continuously discovers, evaluates, and publishes high-signal technology and AI developments in real time."*
  > *"Mexora is fully deployed in production. The React frontend is live on Vercel, and our FastAPI backend service runs autonomously on Render with an active Google Gemini 3.5 Flash decision engine."*

---

### Scene 2: Dashboard Metrics & Autonomous Rejection Grid (0:25 - 0:55)
- 🖥️ **Action**: Scroll down the **Overview** dashboard to show the StatCards and the **Autonomous Rejection & Noise Filter Grid**.
- 🗣️ **Script**: 
  > *"On the Overview dashboard, you can see our real-time metrics: total stories published, active agents, connected RSS feeds, and the state of our dual editorial engine."*
  > *"One of Mexora's core strengths is noise elimination. As shown in our Rejection Grid, out of all candidate RSS stories discovered across AI research labs like OpenAI, DeepMind, and Anthropic, Mexora's editorial engine filters out promotional fluff, low-relevance articles, and duplicate content—maintaining a high noise rejection rate."*

---

### Scene 3: Interactive Pipeline Node Flow Visualizer (0:55 - 1:20)
- 🖥️ **Action**: Hover and click on the nodes in the **Autonomous Pipeline Node Flow Visualizer**.
- 🗣️ **Script**: 
  > *"Here is our interactive 5-stage Autonomous Pipeline Visualizer showing how data flows through the system in real time:"*
  > 1. *"Stage 1 ingests RSS items across 6 research feeds."*
  > 2. *"Stage 2 normalizes URLs and enforces SQLite persistent memory to prevent duplicate coverage."*
  > 3. *"Stage 3 scores candidates on domain relevance and technical depth."*
  > 4. *"Stage 4 evaluates stories through Gemini 3.5 Flash or auto-engages our deterministic fallback engine if API rate limits occur."*
  > 5. *"Stage 5 publishes high-value signals to our dashboard and REST API."*

---

### Scene 4: Live Feed, Score Gauge, Factors & Audio Narration (1:20 - 1:50)
- 🖥️ **Action**: Click on the **Live Feed** tab in the sidebar. Click the **"Listen"** voice briefing button on a post card.
- 🗣️ **Script**: 
  > *"In the Live Feed stream, every published story features a prominent Editorial Alignment Score gauge—for instance, 85 out of 100."*
  > *"Directly below the score, Mexora breaks down its decision into four transparent factors: Domain Fit, Technical Depth, Freshness Index, and Hype Filter."*
  > *"We also integrated browser voice synthesis. Clicking 'Listen' triggers a live audio briefing read in the agent persona's voice with an animated audio equalizer."* *(Let the audio play for 3-5 seconds)*
  > *"You can also filter the stream by individual agent personas like Ada for AI Security or JET for Quantum Computing."*

---

### Scene 5: Interactive AI Editorial Simulator / Sandbox (1:50 - 2:20)
- 🖥️ **Action**: Click on the **Simulator** tab in the sidebar. Select `Ada — AI Security`, paste a test headline into the textarea, and click **"Simulate Editorial Evaluation"**.
- 🗣️ **Sample Headline**: `OpenAI discloses new safety and security evaluation framework for autonomous AI agents`
- 🗣️ **Script**: 
  > *"Let's test our interactive AI Editorial Simulator Sandbox. Judges or users can test any custom news headline against an agent persona in real time."*
  > *"I'll select Ada for AI Security, paste a headline regarding agent safety frameworks, and click 'Simulate Editorial Evaluation'."*
  > *"In real time, the engine analyzes the headline, awards an 88/100 score, displays the factor breakdown bars, issues a PUBLISH decision, and generates a written critique in Ada's persona voice."*

---

### Scene 6: Briefing Export & Hackathon Vibe-Coding Verification (2:20 - 2:40)
- 🖥️ **Action**: Click the **Export Briefing** button in the header. Then switch tabs to your GitHub repository `https://github.com/Geeteshwer/mexora`.
- 🗣️ **Script**: 
  > *"For reporting, users can click 'Export Briefing' to download a complete executive JSON briefing report."*
  > *"Finally, our complete project codebase is open-source on GitHub at Geeteshwer/mexora, complete with PROMPTS.md and AI_USAGE_LOG.md documenting our vibe-coded prompt engineering process."*
  > *"Thank you for watching!"*
